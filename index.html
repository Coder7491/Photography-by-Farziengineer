The Story Behind
"Photography by Farzi Engineer"

Yes, we graduated with engineering degrees. But inside lecture halls and circuit designs, our true obsession was always light, composition, and human emotions.

Founded by The Farzi Engineer alongside Co-Founder Abhimanyu Kumar, our brand earned the moniker from friends when we traded software terminals for 85mm prime lenses and cinematic camera rigs. That engineering rigor became our greatest superpower:

Engineered Technical Precision

Mastery over high dynamic range lighting, color science, and cinematic Sony G-Master optics to capture flawless skin tones in any lighting.

Zero Stiff Poses • Pure Organic Candids

No fake smiles, no cheesy choreographed directions. We blend invisibly into your celebrations so you can live your wedding to the fullest.

Triple-Redundant Data Security

Dual-card recording on shoot, instant on-site backup drives, and cloud archival. Your once-in-a-lifetime moments are 100% safeguarded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking and our photography workflow.

Our founder alongside Co-Founder Abhimanyu Kumar completed formal engineering degrees before dedicating their careers to wedding cinematography, optics, and storytelling. The name is a humorous, affectionate badge: while traditional engineers build machines, we bring rigorous technical precision (color theory, optics, lighting physics, drone cinematography) directly into the art of capturing emotional human stories!

Yes, absolutely! We travel anywhere love takes you — from Udaipur, Jaipur, and Goa to Jim Corbett, Kerala, Mussoorie, and international destination spots. Travel and accommodation logistics are simply included or arranged by the client.

We pride ourselves on punctuality! You receive a curated 48-Hour Sneak Peek (30-50 high-res photos) right after the wedding for your instant Instagram & WhatsApp sharing. The complete color-corrected photo gallery is delivered in 3 weeks, and cinematic teaser films in 4 weeks.

We shoot on industry-leading Sony A7 IV and FX3 full-frame cinema bodies equipped with top-of-the-line G-Master prime lenses, DJI Ronin gimbals, Sennheiser wireless audio microphones, and DJI 4K drones. Every camera shoots dual-card backups simultaneously, and footage is backed up on hard drives the very same night.

Because we only take one wedding per weekend to guarantee 100% personal dedication, dates get booked 4 to 8 months in advance. Dates are reserved on a first-come basis upon signing our agreement and paying a 30% advance deposit.
